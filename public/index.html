PROCESS

Three passes, one request.

Your text or file goes to the Express API, which parses it, runs the same scoring engine every time, and writes the result to SQLite before sending it back.

01

Parse structure

The server extracts text (including from PDFs via pdf-parse) and detects sections, contact details and bullet formatting.

02

Score language

Flags passive phrasing and filler buzzwords, and rewards strong action verbs with measurable outcomes.

03

Persist & compare

Every analysis is saved to the database, so your History tab shows real progress across scans.
